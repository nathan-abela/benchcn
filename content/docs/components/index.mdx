## Readouts

- [Alarm list](/docs/components/alarm-list): rule-driven alarms with acknowledgement and shelving.
- [Battery indicator](/docs/components/battery-indicator): charge from volts or percent, grey until low.
- [Connection status](/docs/components/connection-status): a source's link state, retries and stale detection.
- [Meter readout](/docs/components/meter-readout): multimeter face with hold and min, max and average.
- [Odometry plot](/docs/components/odometry-plot): a robot's path and heading on a scaled grid.
- [Range bar](/docs/components/range-bar): where a value sits against its limits and setpoint, grey until abnormal.
- [Sensor tile](/docs/components/sensor-tile): one reading with thresholds, sparkline and stale detection.
- [Signal strength](/docs/components/signal-strength): bars from RSSI or link quality.

## Controls

- [Actuator control](/docs/components/actuator-control): press-and-hold with commanded versus confirmed state.
- [D-pad](/docs/components/d-pad): stepped jogging with repeat while held and a choice of step size.
- [Emergency stop](/docs/components/emergency-stop): latching software stop with a two-step reset.
- [Joystick](/docs/components/joystick): teleoperation from touch, keys or a gamepad that stops on release.
- [Momentary button](/docs/components/momentary-button): hold to run, off however the hold ends.
- [PWM slider](/docs/components/pwm-slider): streams duty while dragging and confirms the value it was released on.
- [Rotary encoder](/docs/components/rotary-encoder): detented knob with a push button that sends once turning stops.
- [Setpoint input](/docs/components/setpoint-input): numeric setpoint that stays a draft until the device confirms it.

## Displays

- [Character LCD](/docs/components/character-lcd): HD44780 text display from the real character ROM.
- [LED bar](/docs/components/led-bar): segmented bar graph with warning and alarm segments.
- [LED matrix](/docs/components/led-matrix): dot-matrix panel with scrolling text.
- [OLED display](/docs/components/oled-display): monochrome OLED from a bitmap or raw SSD1306 display RAM.
- [Seven-segment display](/docs/components/seven-segment): SVG digits in physical colours.
- [Status LED](/docs/components/status-led): panel LED with patterns.

## Signals

- [Logic analyzer](/docs/components/logic-analyzer): digital lanes with zoom and I²C, SPI, UART and Modbus RTU decode.
- [Oscilloscope](/docs/components/oscilloscope): triggered waveform display with measurements.
- [Strip chart](/docs/components/strip-chart): rolling multi-series trend with honest gaps and CSV export.

## Comms

- [Serial console](/docs/components/serial-console): logs and a command line.

## Boards and docs

- [Bitfield diagram](/docs/components/bitfield-diagram): register layouts with live decoding.
- [Pinout header](/docs/components/pinout-header): board pinouts with wiring and conflict detection.
- [Timing diagram](/docs/components/timing-diagram): WaveJSON-style timing figures.
