title: Overview
description: Whole dashboards built from the components, ready to install and edit.
---

- [Power bench](/docs/blocks/power-bench): a bench supply with confirmed setpoints, CV/CC readback, a meter at the load, a trend and the log.
- [Plant monitor](/docs/blocks/plant-monitor): soil, climate, light, a pump and a console.
- [Weather station](/docs/blocks/weather-station): seven-segment temperature, climate tiles, dew point and pressure trend.

Blocks run on the mock device until you pass a `source`, and every one has a `kiosk` layout for 800×480 touchscreens.
