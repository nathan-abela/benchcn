benchcn components follow your project's shadcn theme. Any base colour and accent works; this site happens to use zinc.

## 1. Chrome follows your theme

Cards, bezels, text, borders and focus rings read the standard shadcn variables: `--background`, `--card`, `--muted`, `--border`, `--primary`, `--ring`. Nothing to configure.

## 2. Channels have their own tokens

Scope channels and logic-analyzer lanes use `--channel-1` to `--channel-4`, which `@benchcn/tokens` adds as bright trace colours that read on the dark screen. A channel without its own token falls back to your `--chart-1` to `--chart-5`, so a fifth lane, or a project that removes the channel tokens, follows your chart palette.

## 3. Physical colours are props

LEDs, LCD backlights and seven-segment digits have fixed colours in the real world, so they take a `color` prop with presets (`red`, `amber`, `green`, `blue`, `white`) and default to `--primary`.

## Status tokens

shadcn ships `--destructive` but no success or warning token. The `@benchcn/tokens` item adds them and every benchcn component depends on it:

```css
:root {
  --success: oklch(62.7% 0.194 149.214);
  --success-foreground: oklch(98.2% 0.018 155.826);
  --warning: oklch(76.9% 0.188 70.08);
  --warning-foreground: oklch(27.9% 0.077 45.635);
}
```

They are mapped into Tailwind as `bg-success`, `text-warning` and so on.

## Reduced motion

Every animation respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Phosphor persistence, spring toggles and press-and-hold fills fall back to instant state changes.
